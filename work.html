AI Spider

An AI retrievability audit platform that extends traditional crawling with citation-readiness analysis. Seven GEO modules, including citation readiness scoring, section strength analysis, HITS-based authority mapping and N-gram analysis, built to replace a commercial crawler license while adding AI-retrievability signals traditional crawlers don't surface.

Shipped

Searchbench

A local SEO workbench that reads the Search Console and Analytics exports you already download, runs thirty-odd checks over them, and shows the arithmetic behind every finding instead of a score. No model touches the analysis, so reading costs nothing and works offline; writing pages runs through your own API key with a ceiling you set. Built and tuned against 345 pages and 1,300 queries.

Shipped

Workbench

A local client website workbench that runs a build across 19 stages, from the first requirements call to the optimisation loop that never ends. Each stage reads what the ones before it decided and ends in a document you can hand over. No progress bars anywhere: status is derived from the data, and the launch decision reads six gates live from the stages that own them.

Shipped
